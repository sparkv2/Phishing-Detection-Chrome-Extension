What the colours mean

Red — known phishing: the sender or a link is on a list of confirmed scams, or you reported it. Don't click, don't reply. Report it.

Yellow — suspicious: it has warning signs but isn't a confirmed scam. Check the sender and links before doing anything.

No colour — no warning signs found.
