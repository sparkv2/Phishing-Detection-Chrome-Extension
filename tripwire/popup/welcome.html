Tripwire is on

It quietly checks your emails for scams and warns you before you click something dangerous.

How it works

  1. Open your email in Chrome (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, and other webmail).
  2. Risky emails in your inbox get coloured:
    Red: known phishing, a confirmed scam.
    Yellow: suspicious, with warning signs but not confirmed. Be careful.
  3. Open any email to see a summary, a safety score from 0 to 100 (100 = safe), and the reasons it was flagged.
  4. If you click a dangerous link, Tripwire stops you first and shows where the link really goes.

Your privacy

Emails are checked on your own computer. Your email content is never uploaded, stored or shared. The only thing downloaded is a public list of known scam websites.

Using a different email website?

Click the Tripwire icon in Chrome's toolbar while your email is open, then press "Check emails on this site".

Five habits that stop most scams

  • Check the sender's full address, not just the name.
  • Hover over links before clicking to see the real destination.
  • Never share passwords or codes by email. Real companies don't ask.
  • Slow down when an email is urgent or scary. That's the trick.
  • Report, don't just ignore. It helps protect everyone.
